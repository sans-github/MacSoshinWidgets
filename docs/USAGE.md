# Using Shoshin

Click the enso (an open circle) in the menu bar. The panel lists every tool, one per row.

Click a tool to open it. The other rows fold away and the tool stays open: next time you click the enso it opens straight to that tool. Click the tool's row again, or "‹ All tools" at the bottom, to see the full list.

## Box Breathing

A wave rises as you breathe in, holds, falls as you breathe out, and holds again: four seconds each. The phase and a countdown sit under the wave. Each time you open it, it starts on an inhale. With Reduce Motion on, the wave keeps its rise and fall but loses the small ripple.

## Focus Block

Pick 15, 25, 50 or 90 minutes and press **start focus**. The pill shows the time left, a bar that fills as the block goes on, and when it ends. The countdown also shows next to the enso in the menu bar, so you can close the panel. A chime plays when the block finishes. **end focus** stops it early. A running block survives quitting and relaunching.
